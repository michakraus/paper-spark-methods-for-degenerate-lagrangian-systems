Poincaré Invariants

Relative error of the first and the second Poincaré invariant,

\[I_{1} = \oint_{\gamma} \vartheta_{i} (q) \, dq^{i} \qquad \text{and} \qquad I_{2} = \int_{S} \omega_{ij} (q) \, dq^{i} \wedge dq^{j} ,\]

over a loop and a surface in configuration space advected by each of the methods above — the problem's own f_loop and f_surface. The surface lies inside the loop but is not the region the loop bounds, so the two are different numbers rather than two estimates of one.

Both use the time step of the example above, over much shorter intervals, since one run advects a few hundred trajectories instead of one. The second invariant runs shorter still: its quadrature interpolates the advected surface, which the flow shears, so beyond the interval shown the figure would report the quadrature failing rather than the method.
